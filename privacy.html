Privacy Policy

Jason InBody Local Sync · Effective 23 October 2026

数据只用于本人读取与归档，保存在本人 Windows 电脑和选定的私有 Google Drive 目录中。健康数据与登录凭据不会发布到此网站或公开仓库。

Scope and operator

This policy covers the personal Jason InBody Local Sync utility and its public documentation website. The utility is operated by its account owner for personal use. Contact the maintainer via the maintainer's public profile; the Google authorization screen also identifies the configured support contact.

Information accessed and purpose

The local utility accesses measurement timestamps, device identification and body-composition measurement fields returned by the owner's InBody account. It accesses the metadata and contents of app-authorized Google Drive archive files to create records, detect duplicates, check privacy and verify saved contents. This information is used only to provide the requested personal retrieval and archiving functions.

Storage and credentials

Measurement JSON files are stored locally on the owner's Windows computer and in selected private Google Drive folders. Raw records are retained without interpreting or repairing uncertain values. A separate core-field archive excludes unconfirmed hidden fields. Local operational logs contain status and error categories, not passwords, tokens or measurement values.

The InBody account password and Google OAuth refresh credentials are stored in Windows Credential Manager. Short-lived access credentials are used in process memory. Credentials are not stored in public source code, measurement archives, this website, or Google Drive.

Disclosure and use limits

The utility sends authorized requests to InBody and Google as needed to retrieve and archive records. It does not publish health records, sell them, use them for advertising or credit decisions, or use them to train generalized AI models. This public website is not an endpoint for collecting health data. Access or analysis separately initiated by the owner in another service is outside this utility's archiving function.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, control and deletion

Archive records are retained until the owner deletes them. The owner can disable Windows scheduled tasks, revoke Google access through Google Account connections, remove local credentials using Windows Credential Manager, and delete local and Drive archives. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing archive copies. Google Drive trash and retention rules apply to files deleted there.

Website hosting

The static documentation is hosted by GitHub Pages. The site includes no analytics, advertising scripts or forms. GitHub may process connection and request information when serving pages under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google and InBody handle requests under their own applicable policies.

Policy updates

Material changes to data access or use will be reflected on this page before the utility requests additional authorization.
